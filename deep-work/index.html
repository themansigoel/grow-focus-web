Deep Work FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use GrowFocus while keeping my phone in Do Not Disturb?

Absolutely. GrowFocus integrates seamlessly with iOS Focus Modes. You can activate your custom Deep Work profile, silencing all Slack, email, and social apps while GrowFocus tracks your uninterrupted session.

Does GrowFocus require creating an account or logging in?

No account required. Download the app and start focusing immediately. Your sessions sync privately across your Apple devices using your native iCloud account.

How does GrowFocus support the 90-minute ultradian rhythm?

You can customize session durations from 10 to 90 minutes. Completing a 90-minute deep work sprint rewards you with increased solar energy and rainwater drops for your botanical sanctuary, incentivizing longer, higher-quality focus blocks.
