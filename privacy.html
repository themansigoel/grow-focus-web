Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Mansi Agarwal ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Focus Timer - GrowFocus mobile application for iOS. Your cognitive wellness and privacy are of utmost importance. GrowFocus is engineered from the ground up to respect your digital boundaries, with zero third-party telemetry, zero user tracking, and 100% on-device data persistence.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection & Tracking

We do not operate external web databases, tracking servers, or user profiling systems. All data you create in GrowFocus — including focus timers, garden biomes, unlocked plant species, streak counts, and cognitive test scores — is stored locally on your device via Apple SwiftData or synced privately through your personal Apple iCloud container.

2. Apple HealthKit Integration

With your explicit permission, GrowFocus integrates with Apple HealthKit to read and write Mindful Sessions.

  • Data Usage: We write completed focus and breathing session durations directly to Apple Health.
  • Privacy Guarantee: Health data is never transmitted to any third party, never uploaded to external servers, and never used for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. StoreKit 2.0 In-App Purchases

All subscription management and lifetime in-app purchases are handled directly by Apple's secure App Store infrastructure. We never receive or store your credit card details, payment addresses, or financial information.

4. On-Device Cognitive Training

All performance measurements in the 10 cognitive game rooms (Hold Focus, Don't Tap, Rule Switch, etc.) are computed in real time on your iPhone's local processor. No biometric or cognitive reaction data is ever shared with external entities.

5. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

If you choose not to subscribe to GrowFocus Pro, non-personalized banner or interstitial advertisements may be served using Google AdMob SDK. AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers solely for ad delivery and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: themansigoel@gmail.com.
