💳 In-App Purchases & StoreKit Restoration

Restoring Purchases: If you upgraded to GrowFocus Pro on another iOS device or reinstalled the app, open GrowFocus > Profile > Settings > Paywall > tap "Restore Purchases".

Subscription Management: Subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime via your iOS Settings > [Your Apple ID] > Subscriptions.
