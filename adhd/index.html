ADHD FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does GrowFocus work better than Forest or standard timers for ADHD?

The attention games offer structured practice and short breaks. GrowFocus is a productivity tool, not a diagnosis or treatment for ADHD or another medical condition.

What if I have severe task initiation paralysis?

Start with a 10-minute adaptive session. Setting a 10-minute timer requires almost zero activation energy from your executive network. Once you begin and see your plant sprout, momentum carries you forward effortlessly.

Does GrowFocus collect or sell any personal or health data?

Core progress stays on-device or in supported Apple services. Firebase Analytics and AdMob also process technical usage and advertising information. Apple Health access requires permission. Read the privacy policy for the details.
