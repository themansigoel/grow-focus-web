ADHD FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does GrowFocus work better than Forest or standard timers for ADHD?

Forest and standard timers rely purely on countdowns. GrowFocus adds 10 neuropsychology-backed cognitive attention gyms (to stimulate your prefrontal cortex before work) and features dynamic 3D Metal biomes with water harvesting and solar tokens for continuous dopamine feedback.

What if I have severe task initiation paralysis?

Start with a 10-minute adaptive session. Setting a 10-minute timer requires almost zero activation energy from your executive network. Once you begin and see your plant sprout, momentum carries you forward effortlessly.

Does GrowFocus collect or sell any personal or health data?

Never. All your focus session history, plant growth, and cognitive scores are stored directly on your iPhone or synced privately via your own iCloud container. Zero external tracking or ads.
