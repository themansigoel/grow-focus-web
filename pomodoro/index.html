Pomodoro FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize the timer intervals beyond 25 minutes?

Yes. While 25 minutes is the classical Pomodoro standard, GrowFocus supports flexible intervals from 10 minutes (for gentle initiation) up to 90 minutes (for full ultradian deep work blocks).

What happens if I get an urgent phone call during a session?

GrowFocus provides a 10-second grace window. Incoming phone calls and quick system interruptions will not wilt your plant as long as you do not abandon the app for prolonged periods.

Are all features free to use?

Yes! The core anti-wilt Pomodoro timer, 3D botanical garden, and attention training gyms are completely free to enjoy with zero third-party advertisements.
